Our Approach

Your care should reflect your life — not a template.

For more than fifteen years, Transformation Health has provided individualized counseling tailored to each person we serve. We take time to understand your history, your family, and your goals, then build a plan of care around them — coordinating therapy, psychiatry, and community support so the pieces work together.

  • Individualized treatmentPlans shaped by your needs, strengths, and pace — not a one-size-fits-all program.
  • Respect for every identityAn affirming, inclusive space for people of every background, family, and community.
  • Community-connected carePartnerships and programs that support recovery well beyond the therapy room.

Community & Enrichment Programs

Support beyond the therapy room.

Alongside clinical treatment, Transformation Health offers creative, vocational, and community programs that help people connect, express themselves, and grow.

Movement

Therapeutic Dance Hall

Movement and expression as a pathway to confidence, connection, and emotional wellness.

Creative

The Healing Arts

Guided creative expression that supports processing, community, and recovery.

Supported Employment

Coaching and workplace support that help individuals build skills, find work, and sustain independence.

LGBTQIA+ Umbrella Club

An affirming space for LGBTQIA+ individuals to find community, belonging, and peer support.

Transformation Health also supports T-Nation, an affiliated 501(c)(3) nonprofit that leads community outreach and enrichment activities. Getting Started

A clear path to your first appointment.

Reaching out is simple, and our intake team will guide you through every step.

1

Reach out or refer

Call our intake team, request care online, or submit a referral. Share as much or as little as you're comfortable with to begin.

2

Speak with the care team

We'll talk through your needs and goals, confirm coverage, and help match you with the right services and location.

3

Begin an individualized plan

You'll be scheduled with a provider and start a plan of care built around you — in person or through telehealth.

Individuals & families

Start the conversation whenever you're ready. Our team can help you understand options, coverage, and next steps.
